# The Legacy Publication — Official Campus App
**University of Rizal System – Morong Campus**

*The Truth To Make A Difference.*

**The Legacy Publication** app brings URS Morong's official student publication together in one place—giving students a clean reading room for campus news, literary folios, photo stories, and mobile journalism broadcasts, while equipping student journalists with a role-based newsroom workspace.

---

## For Readers & URS Students
* **Campus News & Tabloids:** Read breaking campus news, features, and investigative reports anytime.
* **Literary Folios (*Aguhon*):** Browse poetry, prose, and visual art in a mobile-friendly reader with offline saving.
* **Photojournalism & Editorial Art:** View captioned photo essays and editorial cartoons from Legacy artists.
* **TL Frontier & MoJo:** Watch mobile journalism reels and campus broadcast dispatches.
* **Campus Tip Line:** Submit story tips, opinions, or literary contributions directly to the editorial desk.

---

## For Publication Staff
* **Role-Based Newsroom:** Dedicated desks for the Editor-in-Chief, Associate/Section Editors, Writers, Photojournalists, MoJo & Broadcasters, Layout Artists/Cartoonists, and Copyreaders.
* **Weighted Production Tracker:** Live milestone tracking for tabloids and folios across Cover, Write-ups, Artworks, Photos, and Layout.
* **Direct Publishing & Approvals:** Upload photo stories, MoJo videos, and articles with EIC review and account approval.

---

## Download the Android App (APK)
1. Open the **Actions** tab in this GitHub repository.
2. Select the latest **Build The Legacy Publication APK** workflow run.
3. Scroll down to **Artifacts** and download **`the-legacy-publication-debug.apk`**.
